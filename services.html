Residential cleaning

Routine or deep cleaning for homes and apartments, so every kitchen, bathroom, bedroom and living space feels fresh.

  • Routine cleaning: dusting, vacuuming, mopping, trash removal, kitchen surface wipe-downs and bathroom sanitization.
  • Deep cleaning (on demand): inside appliances, cabinet interiors, baseboards, window sills, and grout and tile scrubbing.
  • Optional add-ons: upholstery care, oven and fridge deep clean, inside-window cleaning, and eco-friendly product preference.
  • Flexible schedules: weekly, bi-weekly, monthly or one-off deep cleans, with fully insured staff and a checklist-driven service.

Short-term rental cleaning

Fast, detailed turnovers between guests, so every stay starts with a five-star first impression.

  • Full turnover checklist: change and launder linens, make beds, restock toiletries and guest essentials, and empty trash.
  • Kitchen and bathroom reset: sanitize surfaces, restock consumables, clean appliances and check that amenities work.
  • Staging and inspection: tidy and arrange for photos if requested, report damages, and turn around quickly between bookings.
  • Laundry and records: laundry handling, optional linen rental coordination, and a documented checklist for each stay.

Office cleaning

Professional cleaning for workspaces that keeps desks, meeting rooms and breakrooms ready for a productive day.

  • Workstation care: dusting electronics and desks (no personal items moved), trash and recycling removal, and surface wipe-downs.
  • Common area maintenance: vacuuming and mopping, conference room setup, glass and whiteboard cleaning, and lobby upkeep.
  • Restroom and breakroom sanitation: disinfecting high-touch points, replenishing paper supplies and cleaning appliance exteriors.
  • Flexible plans: daily, nightly or weekend service, with optional supply restocking and green-cleaning options.

Shared space cleaning

Lobbies, hallways, waiting rooms and other communal areas kept tidy, hygienic and welcoming all day.

  • High-touch sanitization: door handles, elevator buttons, handrails, seating areas and reception surfaces.
  • Floor and surface care: sweeping, mopping, vacuuming, spot-cleaning carpets and polishing hard floors.
  • Appearance upkeep: glass and entrance cleaning, trash removal, restocking dispensers and odor control.
  • Scheduled frequency: options to match foot traffic, from daily or several times a day to nightly rotations.

05

Move-in / move-out cleaning

A top-to-bottom clean of a vacant home or apartment, ready for the keys to change hands.

  • Complete deep clean: inside ovens, refrigerators, cabinets and drawers, window tracks and baseboards.
  • Bathroom intensive: descaling showers and tubs, grout cleaning, toilet sanitation, and mirror and fixture polishing.
  • Final touch-ups: dusting vents and light fixtures, debris removal, and paint touch-up recommendations (we don't paint).
  • Inspection support: a move-out checklist and photos on request, for deposit inspections or real-estate handovers.

06

After-construction cleaning

Dust, debris and residue cleared after building or remodeling, leaving the space safe and move-in ready.

  • Rough clean: removing construction debris, large dust build-up and material waste from the site.
  • Fine clean: wiping down every surface, removing drywall dust from fixtures, and cleaning vents, lights and baseboards.
  • Specialty tasks: adhesive and paint-splatter removal, window and glass polishing, and floor finish prep and final polish.
  • Final walkthrough: a checklist-based inspection, touch-up cleaning and sign-off, so the space is ready to occupy.
